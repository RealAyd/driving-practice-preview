Storage on your device

Your browser's storage keeps your results, lesson progress and settings (controller layout, headset calibration, steering, text size, light or dark mode, sound, tutorials seen), an invite code you arrived with until you sign in, and a note while a payment is on its way. It stays on your device; clearing your browser's website data removes it.

Others

Stripe's payment page, and Google's or Apple's sign-in if you use them, set their own cookies on their own sites, under their own policies.
