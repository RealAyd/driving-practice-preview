Cookies and storage

Driving Practice · last updated [date of publication]

Draft for review. Prepared for the owner to check; not legal advice. Items highlighted like this need the owner's details or decision before launch.

Driving Practice uses only what it needs to work, plus its own visit records (how the app is used, to improve it). There are no advertising cookies and no analytics company. [Owner to confirm: visit records rely on the UK exemption for storage used only for statistics to improve a service, with a simple way to say no (Data (Use and Access) Act 2025); check it is in force before launch, or ask for consent instead.]

Storage on your device

Your browser's storage keeps your results, lesson progress and settings (controller layout, headset calibration, steering, text size, light or dark mode, sound, tutorials seen), an invite code you arrived with until you sign in, a note while a payment is on its way, and a random id for the device used in visit records (dp-visitor). Clearing your browser's website data removes it.

Visit records

The app sends its own server which screens were shown and which buttons were pressed, where it was opened from and for how long, with the random device id. Never what you type. None are made when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Others

Stripe's payment page, and Google's or Apple's sign-in if you use them, set their own cookies on their own sites, under their own policies.
