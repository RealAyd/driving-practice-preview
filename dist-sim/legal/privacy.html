Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to move it. Email support.drivingpractice@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), though we would like the chance to put things right first.

Children

Driving Practice is meant for learner drivers aged 16 and over; under-16s should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
