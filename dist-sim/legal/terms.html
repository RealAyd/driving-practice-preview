2. What Driving Practice is

Driving Practice is a driving simulator for practising the judgement, checks and routines used on UK roads: lessons with an instructor's coaching, mock tests with an examiner's directions, and free drives on streets built from map data. It is practice only. It does not replace lessons with an approved driving instructor, is not the DVSA driving test, and is not endorsed by the DVSA. Feedback and results are guidance from our own marking, not an official assessment. Streets are approximate and may differ from the real roads.

3. Using it safely

  • Never use Driving Practice while driving or riding a real vehicle.
  • With a phone headset, stay seated in a clear space, take breaks, and stop at once if you feel dizzy, sick or unwell. Some people should not use headsets (for example with a history of seizures): if in doubt, do not.
  • Under-16s should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.
