Refund policy

Driving Practice · last updated [date of publication]

Draft for review. Prepared for the owner to check; not legal advice. Items highlighted like this need the owner's details or decision before launch.

What happens automatically

  • A drive that fails to load, or that you do not complete: no session is used (a session is used only when a drive is completed).
  • A second Starter Pass on the same account: nothing more is added and the payment is refunded in full automatically.
  • A payment that fails or is cancelled: nothing is taken and nothing is added.

Asking for a refund

  • Unused packs: if you have not completed any drive from a pack, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying it. [Confirm this goodwill policy.]
  • Something went wrong: if drives did not work properly, tell us and we will put it right, with extra sessions or a refund.
  • Small partial refunds (under £5 in total on a payment, for example a promotion code that was not applied) leave your sessions as they are.
  • Larger partial refunds may reduce your unused sessions in proportion; we tell you before we do it.
  • A full refund of a pack takes back its sessions, including any already used, so your balance can go below zero until you top up. The same goes for the Starter Pass.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, normally within 5 to 10 working days. Invite a friend rewards are not taken back if the payment that earned them is refunded.

To ask, email support.drivingpractice@gmail.com with the email address of your account and the date of the payment. Your statutory rights are not affected.
