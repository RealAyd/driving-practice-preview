    Phone headset (experimental)

    Normal touch-screen practice above is unchanged. With the phone in a headset:

    Open Driving Practice from your Home Screen for the headset (in Safari: Share › Add to Home Screen). In the browser its address bar shortens the screen, so the two pictures do not sit under the lenses.

    Parked and not scored: look around, check the mirrors and practise shoulder checks. Menus work by looking at a button for a moment; tap the screen to leave.

    Moving, scored driving needs a physical controller that has passed the check: a Bluetooth game controller or keyboard. Accelerating, braking, signalling and steering are never done by looking at buttons. Do the check before putting the phone in the headset. Steering is Assisted, the Left stick or Steer buttons: choose it on the controller screen, or in the headset with Signal left/right before you start (or while paused).

    Calibrate viewer makes the picture comfortable in your headset: with the phone in it, adjust the settings until the picture is sharp and the two yellow crosses straight ahead look like one. Look at − or + beside a setting for a moment to change it; changes save as you go. Look at Done to finish (or tap the screen). No controller needed.

        Controller check

        Connect a Bluetooth game controller (or keyboard) to this iPhone, then press each control once. This screen shows exactly what Safari receives. If your controller uses a different layout, press Set and then the button you want. A headset remote that pairs over Bluetooth may not appear here at all: many act as a mouse or media keys, which a web page cannot read.

        ControlBound toNowCheck

        Settings are kept on this phone, separately in Safari and in the Home Screen app: set the controller up in the one you will use with the headset.

        Accelerate is held: the car speeds up while you hold it, up to the exercise's assisted speed, and coasts when you let go. Brake acts the moment you press it. Pause opens a menu in the headset; only pressing Pause again resumes.
